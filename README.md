# Naruto — One of my favorite series

**_Naruto_** is a Japanese anime about a young ninja, *Naruto Uzumaki*, who dreams of becoming the Hokage.

---

## Why I Like It
- Inspiring story of hard work and friendship  
- Great battles and unique jutsu  
- Funny and emotional moments

> “Believe it!” — Naruto Uzumaki

---

## Basic Info
- **Genre:** Action, Adventure, Fantasy  
- **Seasons:** 5 (plus *Shippuden*)  
- **Creator:** Masashi Kishimoto
